Understanding Prakruti vs. Vikriti

Your Prakruti is your natural constitution determined at birth, while Vikriti is your current state, which may deviate from your natural balance due to lifestyle, diet, stress, and other factors.

The quiz results reflect your Prakruti (natural constitution). For optimal health, Ayurveda recommends aligning your current state (Vikriti) with your natural constitution (Prakruti).
