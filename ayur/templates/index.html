What is Ayurveda?

Ayurveda is a 5,000-year-old system of natural healing that originated in India. It's not just a medical system but a science of life (Ayur = life, Veda = science or knowledge).

Ayurveda emphasizes prevention and encourages the maintenance of health through close attention to balance in one's life through proper diet, lifestyle, and herbal remedies.

Central to Ayurvedic philosophy is the concept of three doshas: Vata, Pitta, and Kapha. These are biological energies found throughout the human body and mind, and they govern all physical and mental processes.
